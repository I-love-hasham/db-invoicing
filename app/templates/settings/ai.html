Security notice

Anything you send with an AI button leaves your server and goes to Anthropic (the maker of Claude). Don't put passwords, client personal or confidential information, or anything you wouldn't print on an invoice in notes you send. Common passwords, keys, PIN codes and card numbers are blocked automatically, but no filter is perfect — you are the last check. Credentials belong in IT Glue, not in billing notes.

What gets sent

Only the note text you choose to clean up. Whatever you typed in those notes goes with it — including any names — so keep notes to the work done. Nothing else is sent: no amounts, dates or client records.

Notes that look like they contain a password, key, token or card number are refused before anything is sent.

The AI only suggests. Nothing changes until you review the suggestion and click Save.

Keep a monthly spending limit on the key in the Anthropic console ($5 is plenty).
